# Lab Task 1 (Home Activity)

Muhammad Ishfaq · posted Sep 21 · 10 points · due Sep 22, 11:59 PM. Transcribed from `lab-task-1-python-data-structures.png`.

1. A school records student information in a tuple as (name, roll number, grade). Create such a tuple for one student and print their grade.
2. A university keeps track of registered courses using sets. Given two sets of courses (one for Semester 1 and one for Semester 2), display which courses are offered in both semesters.
3. A bookstore keeps its catalog in a dictionary where keys are book titles and values are prices. Create a dictionary of 3 books and display the total cost if a student buys all of them.
4. A company stores employee data (name, department, salary) in a dictionary. Write a program that increases the salary of all employees in the "AI" department by 15%.
5. A shopping cart is stored as a dictionary of items and their prices. Write a function that calculates the total bill after applying a 5% discount if the total exceeds 1000.
